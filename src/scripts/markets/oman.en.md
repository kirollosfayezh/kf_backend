## Omani rules we design for

### E-invoicing: Fawtara from 2027

VAT in Oman is 5% and has applied since 16 April 2021 ([PwC](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/oman/corporate/other-taxes)). Under Decision 189/2026, companies with annual supplies above OMR 5 million must e-invoice from 1 April 2027, and all others from 1 October 2027. Invoices must be XML, issued through a system linked to a provider accredited by the Tax Authority; paper invoices, PDFs and emailed images do not count ([Oman Observer](https://www.omanobserver.om/article/1194193/business/decision-issued-on-tax-invoices-in-electronic-format)).

How Fawtara works, according to the Tax Authority ([Fawtara FAQ](https://tms.taxoman.gov.om/portal/documents/d/taxportal/faq-s)):

- **A five-corner model.** Your system sends each invoice to your accredited provider, which exchanges it with the buyer's provider and reports to the Tax Authority. Providers are tested on the OpenPeppol test bed.
- **Timing.** Business-to-business invoices are exchanged in real time and consumer invoices within 24 hours, with consumer sales starting at the same time as business and government ones.
- **QR code.** Consumer invoices need a QR code generated by the seller, which in practice means your POS.
- **Language and software.** Bilingual invoices are allowed, and businesses can keep their existing ERP.

**Our part.** We are not a provider accredited by the Tax Authority, and we don't claim to be. We prepare your POS or ERP data and connect it to the accredited provider you choose, built to the published specifications. The e-invoicing integration we have running in production is in Egypt: [the Egyptian Tax Authority's e-receipt system](/projects/egypt-eta). Some older material still shows an earlier phased schedule, so check the Tax Authority's Fawtara pages for any update before you fix your plan. More in [e-invoicing in the GCC: where each country stands](/blog/gcc-e-invoicing-by-country).

### Personal data: Royal Decree 6/2022

The Personal Data Protection Law (Royal Decree 6/2022) has been fully enforceable since 5 February 2026, overseen by the Ministry of Transport, Communications and Information Technology. A data protection officer is mandatory, breaches must be reported within 72 hours, and administrative fines reach OMR 2,000 per violation ([CMS](https://cms.law/en/omn/legal-updates/oman-personal-data-protection-law-entering-the-enforcement-phase)). Health, biometric and genetic data needs a permit, and the penalty for unlawfully transferring data abroad can reach OMR 500,000 ([Clyde & Co](https://www.clydeco.com/fr/insights/2022/2/data-protection-law-issued-in-oman)).

We build the consent steps, access logs and alerts a 72-hour notice depends on, and tell you in writing where the database and backups are hosted. This is general information, not legal advice: for a clinic or any service handling health data, check the rules with a lawyer in Oman.

### OmanNet at checkout

OmanNet is the Central Bank of Oman's payment gateway, and 95% of cards in Oman are OmanNet debit cards ([Checkout.com](https://www.checkout.com/payment-methods/accept/omannet)). A checkout that only takes Visa and Mastercard leaves most local cards out. OmanNet reaches your store or app through the payment gateway or bank you contract with, and we connect it through its published API. Shopify Payments is not available in Oman, so a Shopify store there needs a third-party payment provider ([Shopify Help Center](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/payments/shopify-payments/supported-countries)).

Muscat is on UTC+4 all year. Cairo is two hours behind in winter and one hour behind from the last Friday of April to the last Thursday of October ([Ahram Online](https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContentP/1/566547/Egypt/Egypt-to-move-clocks-forward-one-hour-on-Friday-fo.aspx); offsets from the [IANA time zone database](https://www.iana.org/time-zones)).

## Common questions

### Do you have an office in Oman?

No. We are based in Cairo and work with companies in Oman remotely, in the same working week, Sunday to Thursday. The first step is a free 30-minute WhatsApp call, booked in your own time zone.

### When does Fawtara apply to our company?

Under Decision 189/2026: from 1 April 2027 if your annual supplies are above OMR 5 million, and from 1 October 2027 for other companies. Check the Tax Authority's Fawtara pages for any update before you plan.

### Are you an accredited Fawtara provider?

No. The Tax Authority accredits the providers, and we are not one. We prepare your POS or ERP data and connect it to the accredited provider you appoint.

### Do we have to replace our POS or ERP?

Not necessarily. The Tax Authority's FAQ says businesses can keep their existing ERP. Your system does need to produce XML invoice data, a QR code for consumer sales, and a connection to your provider, and we start by reviewing what you run today.

### What are your working hours in Muscat time?

Sunday to Thursday, 11:00 to 19:00 from the last Friday of April to the last Thursday of October, and 12:00 to 20:00 for the rest of the year.

### Who owns the code and the app-store accounts?

Your company does. The code, the database, and the hosting, domain and developer accounts are all in its name, and apps are published under your own Apple and Google accounts.
