AI is useful when it does one clear job inside a process that already exists. It is expensive when it is bought first and a use is found later. We start from the task, test whether AI does it well enough, and only then build it into your systems.

## Where AI works well in a business

- **Turning documents into data**: invoices, receipts, forms and emails into structured records.
- **Classifying and routing**: which department a message belongs to, whether a review is a complaint, which request is urgent.
- **Assistants**: answering customer questions from your own information, on your website or WhatsApp, and handing over to a person when needed.
- **Drafting for review**: first replies, product descriptions and summaries a person checks before sending.
- **Searching your own documents**: finding answers in contracts, policies and past projects.
- **Image analysis**: from virtual try-on in [AI Try-On](/projects/ai-try-on) to detecting fractures in X-rays in [Bone Fracture AI](/projects/bone-fracture-ai).

## How we keep it reliable

- **Test with your real data first.** A short trial on real examples shows how often it is right before anything is built.
- **A person approves what matters.** Prices, payments and decisions with consequences are never fully automatic.
- **Calculations in code, not in the model.** AI extracts the numbers; normal code does the maths.
- **Your data stays under control**, with clear rules on what is sent where.

## How an AI project runs

1. **Pick one task** with high volume and low risk.
2. **Two-week test** on a hundred real examples to measure accuracy.
3. **Build the integration** into your system, with review steps and logs.
4. **Measure and expand** to the next task once the first one pays for itself.

## From first call to launch

The test above decides whether AI is good enough for the task. The project itself runs in the same five stages as all our work:

1. **Discover.** We pick the task, collect real examples from your business and agree what "good enough" means.
2. **Plan.** We decide which AI model to use, what data it sees, where that data goes and where a person reviews the result.
3. **Design.** We design the screens people use: the assistant's chat window, a review queue, or the results shown inside your existing system.
4. **Build.** We build the integration with logs and review steps, and measure accuracy on your examples. You see a working build every one to two weeks.
5. **Launch.** We go live, watch accuracy and running costs, and improve the instructions and rules over time.

## What an AI project costs

Projects start from $200 (about SAR 750 / AED 735), and the final price depends on scope, with a fixed written quote after a free call. For AI work, the price depends mainly on:

- **The task**: sorting messages is simpler than reading many different document layouts or answering from a large library of documents.
- **The accuracy you need**: the higher the stakes, the more testing and review steps the project needs.
- **Integrations**: where the results go, such as your accounting system, CRM, website or WhatsApp.
- **Volume**: AI providers charge per use, so running costs grow with the number of documents or messages.
- **Languages**: supporting Arabic and English means testing on real examples in both.

## Frequently asked questions

### Will AI replace my staff?

In practice it removes the repetitive part of their work — typing, sorting and first drafts — so the same team handles more. Decisions stay with people.

### Is AI expensive to run?

For most business tasks the running cost per document or message is small. We estimate it during the test, before you commit.

### Can the assistant answer in Arabic?

Yes. We build assistants that understand and reply in Arabic and English.

### How long does an AI project take?

The test on real examples comes first, so you know early whether the idea works. After that, the build time depends on the scope and the systems involved, and we share a realistic timeline before we start.

### Where is our data processed and stored?

Your application and database run with the hosting provider and in the region you choose. Before anything is built, we agree which data is sent to the AI model and which stays in your own systems.

### Can you add AI to the system we already have?

Yes. AI usually works best inside the tools your team already uses. We review your existing system, add the AI step with logs and review screens, and maintain it after launch.
