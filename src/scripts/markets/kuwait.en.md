## Kuwait rules we design for

### KNET at checkout

Nearly 80% of online transactions in Kuwait use KNET cards; KNET has 11 member banks and accepts local debit cards ([Checkout.com](https://www.checkout.com/payment-methods/knet)). A checkout that only takes Visa and Mastercard loses a large share of Kuwaiti customers at the last step. KNET reaches your store or app through a payment gateway that supports it, and some gateways offer it only with a direct contract with the local provider (same source). We connect the gateway you choose through its published API.

If you plan to use Shopify, Shopify Payments is not available in Kuwait, so the store needs a third-party payment provider ([Shopify Help Center](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/payments/shopify-payments/supported-countries)). Check that it supports KNET before you commit to the platform.

### Personal data: CITRA Decision 26/2024

CITRA's Administrative Decision 26 of 2024 defines a "service provider" broadly enough to include anyone running a website, app or cloud service that collects personal data. Terms must be given in Arabic and English, consent is required, and breaches must be reported to CITRA and to the people affected within 24 hours. Penalties reach one to five years in prison and/or KWD 500 to 20,000 ([DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?t=law&c=KW)).

We build the consent steps, the bilingual terms pages and the logging a 24-hour breach notice depends on from the first release. This is general information, not legal advice: have a Kuwaiti lawyer review your final policy text. More in [building a website or app for Kuwait: KNET and CITRA](/blog/website-app-development-kuwait).

### No VAT and no e-invoicing yet

Kuwait has no VAT; the GCC VAT framework is still under discussion in the National Assembly ([PwC](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kuwait/corporate/other-taxes)). We found no e-invoicing mandate or official announcement when we checked on 25 November 2026.

So a Kuwaiti store or booking app needs no VAT lines and no tax-authority integration today. If you also sell in Saudi Arabia, the UAE or Oman, we keep tax settings per country rather than hard-coding one. See [e-invoicing in the GCC: where each country stands](/blog/gcc-e-invoicing-by-country).

### Digital identity

The Public Authority for Civil Information's Kuwait Mobile ID app is a digital civil ID, used for identity verification, for signing in to government and non-government e-services, and for digital signatures ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1449712307)). If your service needs to verify who a user is, confirm with PACI how a private service can use it before the feature is planned.

### Delivery apps

talabat describes itself as the category leader across its eight markets, Kuwait among them ([talabat](https://ir.talabat.com/)), and Keeta launched in Kuwait on 15 September 2025 ([TechNode](https://technode.com/2025/09/16/meituan-expands-food-delivery-brand-keeta-to-kuwait/)). If you sell through them and through your own site or app, we plan from the start how orders from each channel reach the kitchen or the stockroom.

## How we work with you from Cairo

### The same working week

Kuwait's official weekend has been Friday and Saturday since 1 September 2007 ([Asharq Al-Awsat](https://en-archive.aawsat.com/kuwait-switches-to-friday-saturday-weekend/)). We work Sunday to Thursday, 10:00 to 18:00 Cairo time, so our working days are yours.

### Kuwait time

Kuwait is on UTC+3 all year. Cairo is on the same time from the last Friday of April to the last Thursday of October and one hour behind for the rest of the year ([Ahram Online](https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContentP/1/566547/Egypt/Egypt-to-move-clocks-forward-one-hour-on-Friday-fo.aspx); offsets from the [IANA time zone database](https://www.iana.org/time-zones)).

## Common questions

### Do you have an office in Kuwait?

No. We are based in Cairo and work with companies in Kuwait remotely, in the same working week, Sunday to Thursday. The first step is a free 30-minute WhatsApp call, booked in your own time zone.

### Do we need KNET if we already accept Visa and Mastercard?

For most consumer businesses, yes. Nearly 80% of online transactions in Kuwait use KNET cards, so a checkout without it turns away many customers at the last step.

### Does CITRA Decision 26/2024 apply to a company website?

Its definition of a service provider is broad enough to cover anyone running a website or app that collects personal data. If you collect names, phone numbers or emails, plan Arabic and English terms, consent and a breach procedure, and confirm the details with a lawyer.

### Do we need VAT or e-invoicing on our store?

Not as of 25 November 2026. Kuwait has no VAT, and we found no e-invoicing mandate or official announcement. If you sell in other Gulf countries too, we build tax settings per country.

### What are your working hours in Kuwait time?

Sunday to Thursday, 10:00 to 18:00 from the last Friday of April to the last Thursday of October, and 11:00 to 19:00 for the rest of the year.

### Who owns the code and the app-store accounts?

Your company does. The code, the database, and the hosting, domain and developer accounts are all in its name, and apps are published under your own Apple and Google accounts.
