## From discovery to launch

Every website goes through the same five stages, and during development you review a working build every one to two weeks on your own phone.

1. **Discover.** A free call about your clients, your services and what a visitor should do: call, send a WhatsApp message, request a quote or buy.
2. **Plan.** The sitemap, the pages per language, the content each page needs and who writes it, plus any store or booking features, agreed in writing.
3. **Design.** Mobile and desktop layouts in Arabic and English that you approve before development.
4. **Build.** Development, the content dashboard, technical SEO and analytics, tested on real phones and browsers.
5. **Launch.** Domain, hosting, SSL, Google Search Console and analytics go live, followed by monitoring, updates and new pages.

Projects start from $200, and the final price depends on scope. As a web development company in Egypt, we give you a fixed written quote after a free call, so you know the cost before work begins. For a website, the price is driven mainly by:

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does a website take?

A focused company website typically takes two to four weeks once the content is ready. A custom-designed bilingual site with a dashboard usually takes six to twelve weeks. Web applications take longer and are scoped separately.

### Will my website appear on Google?

We build every site with technical SEO in place and submit it to Google Search Console. Ranking for competitive searches also depends on content and links over time, and we can help with both.

### Can I edit the website myself?

Yes. Every site comes with a dashboard where you can change text, photos, services, projects and articles in each language.

### Do you handle hosting and the domain?

Yes. We set up the domain, hosting and SSL, and the accounts are registered in your name.

### Can you redesign or take over our current website?

Yes. We can audit your existing site, fix what is broken, extend it or rebuild it, and take over the code from a previous developer. Before we quote, we tell you honestly whether a rebuild is worth it.

### Do you offer website maintenance after launch?

Yes. After launch we handle updates, fixes, new pages and hosting monitoring, while you edit content yourself and come to us for larger changes.

### Can the website connect to WhatsApp, payments or our other systems?

Yes. We integrate payment gateways, messaging such as WhatsApp, maps, analytics and third-party APIs in most projects, so enquiries and orders reach the right place.
