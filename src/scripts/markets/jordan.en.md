## Jordanian rules we design for

### E-invoicing: JoFotara

Jordan charges a general sales tax, similar in operation to VAT, at a standard rate of 16% ([PwC](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/corporate/other-taxes)). Since 1 April 2025, invoices for goods and services must be issued through the national e-invoicing system, JoFotara, or a platform integrated with it; non-compliant invoices are invalid for tax deductions, and each invoice carries a unique QR code issued by the Income and Sales Tax Department ([VATupdate](https://www.vatupdate.com/2025/07/17/jordans-e-invoicing-phase-2-mandatory-compliance-begins-april-1-2025/)).

For a POS or ERP this means each sale is sent to JoFotara, validated, and returned with its QR code before the invoice is final. We build that connection into your system, with a status kept for every invoice, a queue that retries when the internet drops, and the QR code on the printed or digital receipt. The e-invoicing integration we have running in production is in Egypt: [the Egyptian Tax Authority's e-receipt system](/projects/egypt-eta), where each receipt is submitted and its status kept with the sale. Confirm with the Income and Sales Tax Department, or your accountant, how the rules apply to your business.

### Personal data: Law 24 of 2023

Jordan's Personal Data Protection Law No. 24 of 2023 came into effect on 17 March 2024, with a 12-month compliance period ([Ministry of Digital Economy and Entrepreneurship](https://modee.gov.jo/EN/Pages/FAQs)). After a serious breach, the controller must notify affected people within 24 hours and the Unit at the ministry within 72 hours ([DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?c=JO&t=breach-notification)). Fines run from JOD 1,000 to 10,000 per breach of the law, and can be doubled for a repeat ([Clyde & Co](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2023/10/jordan-issues-first-personal-data-protection-law)).

We build the consent steps, access logs and alerts those deadlines depend on, and tell you in writing where the database and backups are hosted. This is general information, not legal advice: check your policy text with a lawyer in Jordan.

### Payments: CliQ, mobile wallets and eFAWATEERcom

JoPACC runs Jordan's instant payment systems: CliQ for instant transfers from a bank account, JoMoPay for mobile wallet transfers, and eFAWATEERcom for bill payments ([JoPACC](https://www.jopacc.com/)). CliQ passed 2 million users in October 2025 (same source). Cards reach your store or app through a payment gateway or bank you contract with; if you want customers to pay by CliQ or through eFAWATEERcom, plan it with that provider before the checkout is designed. Shopify Payments is not available in Jordan, so a Shopify store there needs a third-party payment provider ([Shopify Help Center](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/payments/shopify-payments/supported-countries)).

### Digital identity

Sanad is the government app of the Ministry of Digital Economy and Entrepreneurship, and more than 3 million digital identities have been activated through it ([Ministry of Digital Economy and Entrepreneurship](https://www.modee.gov.jo/En/NewsDetails/3_Million_Digital_Identities_Activated_Through_the_%E2%80%9CSanad%E2%80%9D_App)). If your service needs to verify who a user is, confirm with the ministry what a private service can use before the feature is planned.

### Delivery apps

talabat describes itself as the category leader across its eight markets, Jordan among them ([talabat](https://ir.talabat.com/)). If you sell through it and through your own site or app, we keep one menu and one stock figure, usually from your POS, so orders from each channel reach the kitchen or the stockroom in one place.

## How we work with you from Cairo

### The same working week

Jordan's weekend is Friday and Saturday, and its working week Sunday to Thursday ([The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/mena/2021/12/07/when-is-the-weekend-in-the-arab-world)). We work Sunday to Thursday, 10:00 to 18:00 Cairo time, so our working days are yours.

### Amman time

Jordan has stayed on UTC+3 all year since the government decided in October 2022 to keep summer time permanently ([IANA time zone mailing list](https://lists.iana.org/hyperkitty/list/tz@iana.org/thread/FEG6YIOUCFQOBEIOZOTJ4GF5UNIZDD33/)). Cairo is on the same time from the last Friday of April to the last Thursday of October and one hour behind for the rest of the year ([Ahram Online](https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContentP/1/566547/Egypt/Egypt-to-move-clocks-forward-one-hour-on-Friday-fo.aspx); offsets from the [IANA time zone database](https://www.iana.org/time-zones)).

| Last Friday of April to last Thursday of October | 10:00–18:00 |
| Rest of the year | 11:00–19:00 |

## Common questions

### Do you have an office in Jordan?

No. We are based in Cairo and work with companies in Jordan remotely, in the same working week, Sunday to Thursday. The first step is a free 30-minute WhatsApp call, booked in your own time zone.

### Can your POS issue invoices through JoFotara?

Yes. We build the connection so each sale is sent to JoFotara, validated and returned with its QR code, with a status kept for every invoice and a retry queue for when the internet drops. If you already run a POS or ERP, we start by reviewing it.

### Does Jordan's data protection law apply to our website?

If your website or app collects personal data such as names, phone numbers or emails, plan for consent, a breach procedure that meets the 24-hour and 72-hour deadlines, and clear terms. Confirm the details with a lawyer in Jordan.

### Which payment methods should our store accept?

Local and international cards through your gateway, and, if your customers expect them, CliQ transfers or bill payment through eFAWATEERcom. The provider you contract with decides how each method is connected, so choose it before the checkout is designed.

### What are your working hours in Amman time?

Sunday to Thursday, 10:00 to 18:00 from the last Friday of April to the last Thursday of October, and 11:00 to 19:00 for the rest of the year.

### Who owns the code and the app-store accounts?

Your company does. The code, the database, and the hosting, domain and developer accounts are all in its name, and apps are published under your own Apple and Google accounts.
