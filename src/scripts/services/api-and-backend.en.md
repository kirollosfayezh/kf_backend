Every app, website and business system depends on a backend: the server code, database and integrations that store data, enforce rules and talk to other systems. When the backend is weak, everything built on it becomes slow, fragile or insecure.

We design and build backends that are secure, fast and easy to extend.

## What we build

- **REST APIs** for web and mobile apps, documented for your team or partners.
- **Integrations** with payment gateways, delivery companies, SMS and WhatsApp, accounting systems and government platforms such as the [Egyptian Tax Authority e-receipt system](/projects/egypt-eta).
- **Databases** designed for your data, with backups and migrations.
- **Authentication and permissions**: accounts, roles, admin access and audit logs.
- **Real-time features**: live updates, chat and notifications.
- **Background jobs**: scheduled tasks, imports, exports and automations.

## Built to last

- **Security first**: encrypted connections, safe password handling, rate limits and least-privilege access.
- **Performance**: caching, efficient queries and scaling as traffic grows.
- **Monitoring**: error tracking, uptime checks and logs, so problems are found before users report them.
- **Documentation and tests**, so another developer can pick it up.
- **Hosting** on reliable cloud infrastructure, in accounts you own.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you connect our system to another company's API?

Yes. Integrations are a large part of our work — payments, shipping, accounting, government platforms and partner systems.

### Can you take over an existing backend?

Yes. We start with a review of the code, security and hosting, fix the urgent issues, then continue development.

### Where will the backend be hosted?

On established cloud providers, chosen for your budget and traffic, with the accounts in your company's name.
