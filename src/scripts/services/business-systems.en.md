When a business grows, it usually runs on a mix of spreadsheets, paper, a POS nobody likes and WhatsApp groups. Each works on its own. Together they lose stock, hide mistakes and make month-end painful.

We build business systems that bring those pieces into one place — so the owner, the manager and the cashier all see the same numbers.

## Systems we build

- **POS systems** for restaurants and retail: orders, tables, kitchen screens, cashiers, shifts and receipts.
- **Inventory and warehouse**: stock levels, transfers between branches, purchase orders and waste tracking.
- **ERP modules**: sales, purchasing, accounting exports and approvals connected in one system.
- **HR and operations**: attendance, tasks, requests and staff performance.
- **Dashboards and reports**: live figures per branch, product and employee, exported to Excel or PDF.

A ready-made POS or ERP is often the right start for a small business. A custom system makes sense when your workflow doesn't fit a package, when you are paying for several tools that don't connect, or when you need reports no package gives you. ## How an ERP or POS project runs

Every system goes through the same five stages, and during the build you see a working version every one to two weeks.

1. **Discover.** A free call, then time with the people at the counter, in the warehouse and in accounts, to see where stock, cash and paperwork go wrong today.
2. **Plan.** The modules for the first phase, branches, roles and permissions, hardware, the ETA e-invoice or e-receipt integration if you need it, and the data to migrate, in a written scope with a fixed price.
3. **Design.** Cashier, manager and owner screens, receipts and reports in Arabic and English, reviewed with your team.
4. **Build.** Short cycles, with each working build tested on your products, prices and staff.
5. **Launch.** Data migration, training and go-live, starting with one branch if you prefer, followed by monitoring and new modules.

## What an ERP or POS system costs

Projects start from $200. The final price depends on scope, and as an ERP and POS software development company in Egypt we give you a fixed written quote after a free call. The main cost drivers are:

- **Modules.** POS only, or POS with inventory, purchasing, HR and accounting exports.

- **ETA integration.** Signing and submitting e-invoices or e-receipts and tracking their status.
- **Hardware.** Receipt printers, barcode scanners, scales, cash drawers and kitchen screens.
- **Offline mode and data migration** from your current system or spreadsheets.

Starting with the module that hurts most and adding the rest in phases keeps the first bill small. Read [ERP for small businesses in Egypt](/blog/erp-for-small-business-egypt) and [how much custom software costs in Egypt](/blog/custom-software-cost-egypt) before you compare quotes.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can the system work offline?

Yes, where it matters. POS screens can keep taking orders during an internet cut and sync when the connection returns.

### Can you integrate with the ETA e-invoice system?

Yes. We have built ETA e-receipt integrations that sign and submit each receipt and keep its submission status with the sale.

### Can we move our existing data?

Yes. We import products, customers, suppliers and opening stock from your current system or spreadsheets.

### Is it one system for all branches?

Yes. Branches share one system, each with its own stock and staff, and management sees everything in one dashboard.

### How long does an ERP or POS project take?

It depends on the modules, branches and integrations, so we share a realistic timeline in the written scope before we start. Larger systems go live in phases, and you see a working build every one to two weeks.

### Can you extend or take over the system we already use?

Yes. If your current system was custom built, we can audit it, fix it, extend it and maintain it. If it is a ready-made package that allows integration, we can connect new modules or reports to it instead of replacing it.

### Do you support the system after go-live?

Yes. We monitor the system, fix issues and add modules as you grow. Support does not stop at go-live.
