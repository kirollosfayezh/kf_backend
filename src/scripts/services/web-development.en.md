Your website is usually the first thing a client sees, and on a phone they decide in a few seconds whether to stay. We build websites and web applications that load fast, read well in Arabic and English, and are built so Google can find them.

## What we build

- **Company websites** that explain what you do and turn visits into calls, WhatsApp messages and quote requests.
- **Online stores** with product catalogues, carts and local payment and delivery options.
- **Web applications**: customer portals, booking systems, dashboards and internal tools that run in the browser.
- **Landing pages** for campaigns, built to be measured.

Recent examples include the export platform for [Alkhameesi](/projects/alkhameesi), a luxury real-estate site for [Solana Red Sea](/projects/solana-red-sea) and a towing-service platform for [Eltayib](/projects/eltayib).

## Built in, not added later

- **SEO from day one.** Clean page structure, proper titles and descriptions, structured data, a sitemap and fast loading — the technical basics Google ranks on.
- **Speed.** Optimised images, modern code and caching, so pages load quickly even on mobile data.
- **Arabic and English.** Proper right-to-left layouts, not a mirrored afterthought, with separate pages per language for search.
- **You control the content.** A dashboard to edit text, images, projects, articles and offers without calling a developer.
- **Measurement.** Analytics and conversion tracking set up so you know which pages and campaigns bring clients.

## How a website project runs

1. **Goals and content.** Who the site is for, what they should do on it, and what pages that needs.
2. **Design.** Layouts for mobile and desktop that you review before development.
3. **Development.** Built on a modern stack, tested on real phones and browsers.
4. **Launch.** Domain, hosting, security certificates, Google Search Console and analytics.
5. **After launch.** Updates, new pages and performance checks.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does a website take?

A company website typically takes three to six weeks, depending on the number of pages and how ready the content is. Web applications take longer and are scoped separately.

### Will my website appear on Google?

We build every site with technical SEO in place and submit it to Google Search Console. Ranking for competitive searches also depends on content and links over time, and we can help with both.

### Can I edit the website myself?

Yes. Every site comes with a dashboard where you can change text, photos, services, projects and articles in each language.

### Do you handle hosting and the domain?

Yes. We set up the domain, hosting and SSL, and the accounts are registered in your name.
