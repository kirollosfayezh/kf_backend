A mobile app is only as good as the system behind it. We build the app your customers or staff install, and the backend, database and admin dashboard that make it work.

## What we build

- **Customer apps**: ordering, booking, loyalty, tracking and account management.
- **Field and staff apps**: task lists, check-ins, photos and forms that sync when the connection comes back.
- **Companion apps for connected devices**, such as the live generator map in [GenTrack](/projects/gentrack) and the real-time weight readings in [Smart Balance](/projects/smart-balance).
- **Admin dashboards** so your team can manage users, content and orders behind the app.

## One codebase, two platforms

We build with cross-platform technology, so iOS and Android share one codebase. That means one team, one set of features and updates that reach both stores at the same time — usually at a lower cost than two separate native apps, without giving up a native feel.

## What every app includes

- Secure sign-in and user accounts.
- A backend API and database built for the app.
- Push notifications.
- Offline support where the app needs it.
- Analytics and crash reporting.
- App Store and Google Play publishing, including store listings.

## How an app project runs

1. **Scope.** The screens, user types and features for the first release, agreed in writing.
2. **Design.** Clickable screens you can try on your phone before development.
3. **Build.** App, backend and dashboard developed together, with test builds you install as we go.
4. **Store release.** Review, publishing and the listing for each store.
5. **After launch.** Updates for new OS versions, fixes and new features.

## From discovery to launch

Every app goes through the same five stages, from the first call to the store release.

1. **Discover.** A free call about who will use the app, what they need to do on their phone and what has to happen behind it: orders, payments, staff, devices.
2. **Plan.** User types, screens, the backend and admin dashboard, integrations and offline needs, written down with a fixed price for the first release.
3. **Design.** Clickable screens in Arabic and English that you try on your phone and change before development.
4. **Build.** App, backend and dashboard developed together. You install a working build every one to two weeks, so you test real features, not mock-ups.
5. **Launch.** Store submission, review and listings, backend deployment, then monitoring and updates based on how people use the app.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does it take to build an app?

A focused first version usually takes eight to fourteen weeks, including the backend and dashboard. We recommend launching a smaller first version and growing it with real user feedback.

### Do I need both an app and a website?

Not always. If your users are mostly on phones and need notifications, offline use or device features, an app makes sense. Otherwise a fast mobile website can be cheaper and reach more people. We will recommend the right one for your case.

### Who publishes the app to the stores?

We handle the submission and review process. The developer accounts are created in your company's name so you own the app listing.

### Can the app work in Arabic?

Yes. Apps are built with full right-to-left Arabic support alongside English.

### Can you take over an app another team built?

Yes. We review the existing code, backend and store accounts, fix what is blocking you, and then maintain and extend the app. If parts are not worth keeping, we tell you before we quote.

### What happens after the app is live?

We keep monitoring crashes and the backend, release updates when Apple and Google ship new OS versions, and add features based on real user feedback. Support does not stop at launch.

### Can the app connect to devices or our existing systems?

Yes. We have built apps that read live data from connected hardware, like [GenTrack](/projects/gentrack) and [Smart Balance](/projects/smart-balance), and we integrate payment gateways, maps, messaging and existing APIs.
