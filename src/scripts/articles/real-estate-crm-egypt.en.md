Real estate companies in Egypt spend heavily on ads, and most of the leads those
ads bring in are never followed up properly. A form fill from Facebook lands in
a spreadsheet nobody opens until the next morning. A WhatsApp enquiry sits on one
salesperson's personal phone. A client who called twice is entered twice by two
agents. And when a salesperson leaves, their clients leave with them.

A real estate CRM fixes this by putting every lead, every conversation and every
unit in one system the whole sales team works from.

## Where the leads get lost

- **Slow first replies.** A buyer who filled in a form is comparing several
  developers that same evening. The one who calls first has an advantage.
- **No follow-up.** Most sales need several conversations. Without reminders,
  the second call never happens.
- **Scattered channels.** Ad forms, the website, WhatsApp, phone calls, portals
  and walk-ins all end up in different places.
- **Duplicates and disputes.** Two agents contact the same client, or argue about
  whose client it is.
- **No link from sale to campaign.** Marketing knows the cost per lead, not the
  cost per sale.

## What a real estate CRM should do

### Capture every lead automatically

Leads from ad forms, the website, landing pages and WhatsApp should arrive in
the CRM by themselves, with the campaign and project they came from. Phone calls
and walk-ins are added in seconds. The system recognises a returning phone
number and adds the new enquiry to the existing client instead of creating a
duplicate.

### Assign and remind

Each new lead goes to a salesperson by clear rules: by project, by rotation or
by language. The salesperson gets a task to call, and the manager sees any lead
that has waited too long without contact.

### A pipeline everyone understands

Clients move through clear stages, for example: new, contacted, interested,
site visit, reservation, contract, and lost with a reason. Managers see how many
clients are in each stage, for each project and each salesperson, and where
deals stall.

> The reasons deals are lost are worth as much as the deals themselves. Make
> recording them a required step.

### Units and availability

Sales staff should see the live status of every unit: project, building, floor,
area, view, price, and whether it is available, on hold or sold. Two agents
should never be able to reserve the same unit, and a hold should expire
automatically if no deposit arrives.

### Payment plans

Off-plan sales in Egypt usually come with long payment plans: a down payment,
then instalments over several years, sometimes with extra payments on delivery.
The CRM should generate the schedule for each sale, show what is due and
overdue, and remind clients and the collections team.

### Brokers and commissions

If you sell through brokers, record which broker brought which client, protect
that registration for an agreed period, and calculate commissions when a sale
closes. Disputes with brokers usually come from having no record at all.

## WhatsApp and calls

Most conversations with buyers happen on WhatsApp. The CRM should hold those
conversations on company numbers, connected to the client record, so the history
stays when a salesperson leaves. It is the same official WhatsApp platform we

## Reports that change decisions

- **Cost per lead and cost per sale**, by campaign and platform
- **Speed to first contact**, by salesperson
- **Conversion by stage**, to see where clients drop out
- **Units sold and remaining**, by project, with expected revenue
- **Collections**: instalments due, paid and overdue

A campaign with cheap leads that never buy is expensive. Only a CRM linked to
sales shows that. Sending ad traffic to a focused page also helps; see
[landing pages for ads in Egypt](/blog/landing-page-for-ads-egypt).

## Common questions

### Can leads from Facebook and Instagram ads reach the CRM directly?

Yes. Lead forms can be connected so that every new lead appears in the CRM within
moments, tagged with the campaign it came from, and assigned to a salesperson.

### Will salespeople actually use it?

Only if it saves them time. Calling, messaging and logging an outcome should take
fewer taps than their current notebook. A mobile app for the sales team helps a
lot.

### What happens to client data when a salesperson leaves?

The clients, conversations and history stay with the company, and the manager
reassigns them. This is one of the main reasons companies move to a CRM.

### How long does it take to set up?

Capturing leads, assignment and the pipeline can be running in a few weeks. Unit
inventory, payment plans and broker commissions take longer, depending on how
many projects and rules you have.
