## Kuwait

- **Tax.** Kuwait has no VAT; the GCC VAT framework is still under discussion in the National Assembly ([PwC](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kuwait/corporate/other-taxes)). We found no e-invoicing mandate when we checked on 6 November 2026.
- **Payments.** Nearly 80% of online transactions in Kuwait use KNET cards; KNET has 11 member banks and accepts local debit cards ([Checkout.com](https://www.checkout.com/payment-methods/knet)). Some gateways, Checkout.com among them, offer KNET as a gateway-only method that needs a direct contract with the local provider (same source).
- **Data protection.** CITRA's Administrative Decision 26 of 2024 defines a "service provider" broadly enough to include anyone running a website, app or cloud service that collects personal data. Terms must be given in Arabic and English, consent is required, and breaches must be reported to CITRA and to the people affected within 24 hours. Penalties reach one to five years in prison and/or KWD 500 to 20,000 ([DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?t=law&c=KW)).
- **Delivery apps.** Alongside talabat, Keeta launched in Kuwait on 15 September 2025 ([TechNode](https://technode.com/2025/09/16/meituan-expands-food-delivery-brand-keeta-to-kuwait/)).

What this means for a Kuwait project: Arabic and English terms and consent screens from the first release, a breach procedure that meets the 24-hour deadline, and KNET at checkout. More in [Building a website or app for Kuwait: KNET and CITRA](/blog/website-app-development-kuwait).

## Qatar

- **Tax.** Qatar has no VAT; excise tax has applied since 1 January 2019 ([PwC](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/qatar/corporate/other-taxes)).
- **E-invoicing.** On 6 May 2026 the Cabinet approved a draft e-invoicing law and its executive regulations, prepared by the Ministry of Finance with the General Tax Authority ([QNA](https://qna.org.qa/en/news/news-details?id=cabinet-holds-regular-meeting&date=6%2F05%2F2026)). We found no evidence that it has been enacted, and no dates for when it applies.
- **Payments.** Himyan is the national card: the debit version launched on 30 March 2024 and runs on NAPS, and online shopping goes through the QPay portal ([QNA](https://qna.org.qa/en/news/news-details?id=0019-qatar-central-bank-launches-%27himyan%27-debit-card&date=30%2F03%2F2024)). Qatar Central Bank planned to launch Fawran, a 24/7 instant transfer service, in March 2024 ([QNA](https://www.qna.org.qa/en/News-Area/News/2024-02/19/0030-qcb-to-launch-instant-payment-service-%27fawran%27-in-march)).
- **Data protection.** Law 13 of 2016 is overseen by the NCGAA, part of the National Cyber Security Agency. Breaches must be reported within 72 hours, sensitive data needs authorisation and a data protection impact assessment, fines reach QAR 5 million (about 1.4 million US dollars), and the Qatar Financial Centre has its own regime ([DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/countries/qatar/law.html)).
- **Delivery apps.** Alongside talabat, Keeta has operated in Doha since 19 August 2025 ([imsilkroad.com](https://en.imsilkroad.com/p/347199.html)), and Snoonu is now majority-owned by Saudi Arabia's Jahez, which completed a 76.56% stake in October 2025 ([Bridge MENA](https://bridgemena.com/news/jahez-completes-second-phase-of-acquisition-in-snoonu)).

More in [Website and app projects in Qatar: what's different](/blog/website-app-development-qatar).

## Bahrain

- **Tax.** VAT is 10% since 1 January 2022, administered by the National Bureau for Revenue, and registration is mandatory above BHD 37,500 ([Bahrain national portal](https://www.bahrain.bh/wps/portal/en/BNP/HomeNationalPortal/ContentDetailsPage/!ut/p/z0/lY9BT8JAEIX_ynpoogezQ6uAR5GEaoKgJgh7IdN2LCPtbOluG36-LfFA5ORxJu99-Z42eq2NYMs5eraCRXdvzHA7eL6HePwEsJzehfC2engfTuYDmM8i_UGiX7Q5D8WvyykMF_F4FI4m0WIV9RT-PhzMozapFU9Hr9eJVFuSADCxjVd-R2rPkme2DCDBXY0sKoQIAqipQE-Z8rbi1AWQN5x1SVXVLClXBTllv04A6uC25FS17Dr9U_-v3OWC_8o1joWcUyiZYmnJ-ZLEX3q2WDSkMMv6Hx7VdYv-pmuc43rz37W62s8-b83m6gcBLJvw/)).
- **E-invoicing.** Expected, under a framework similar to Saudi Arabia's, but with no official date ([PwC](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/bahrain/corporate/other-taxes)).
- **Payments.** BENEFIT runs Bahrain's electronic funds transfer system, including Fawri+ (24/7 transfers), Fawri and Fawateer (bill payment) ([Central Bank of Bahrain](https://www.cbb.gov.bh/payment-settlement/)). Its BenefitPay app handles QR payments at merchants, Fawri+ transfers and Fawateer bills ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/bh/app/id1249328388)).
- **Data protection.** The Personal Data Protection Law (Law 30 of 2018), in force since 1 August 2019, allows transfers to 83 listed jurisdictions or on other legal grounds, requires breach notice within 72 hours, and carries penalties of up to one year in prison and/or BHD 1,000 to 20,000 ([DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/countries/bahrain/law.html)).
- **Delivery apps.** Alongside talabat, Keeta launched in Bahrain on 5 January 2026 ([EqualOcean](https://equalocean.com/news/2026010721712-meituans-keeta-launches-food-delivery-service-bahrain)).

More in [Website and app projects in Bahrain: payments, VAT, data](/blog/website-app-development-bahrain).

## Oman

- **Tax.** VAT is 5% since 16 April 2021 ([PwC](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/oman/corporate/other-taxes)).
- **E-invoicing (Fawtara).** Under Decision 189/2026, companies with annual supplies above OMR 5 million must e-invoice from 1 April 2027, and all others from 1 October 2027. Invoices must be XML, issued through a system linked to a provider accredited by the Tax Authority; paper invoices, PDFs and emailed images do not count. A voluntary pilot started at the end of August 2026 ([Oman Observer](https://www.omanobserver.om/article/1194193/business/decision-issued-on-tax-invoices-in-electronic-format)).
- **How Fawtara works.** It uses a 5-corner model, and service providers are tested on the OpenPeppol test bed. Business-to-business invoices are exchanged in real time and consumer invoices within 24 hours, with consumer sales starting at the same time as business and government ones. Consumer invoices need a QR code generated by the seller, bilingual invoices are allowed, and businesses can keep their existing ERP ([Tax Authority Fawtara FAQ](https://tms.taxoman.gov.om/portal/documents/d/taxportal/faq-s)).
- **Our part.** We are not a provider accredited by the Tax Authority. We prepare your POS or ERP data and connect it to the accredited provider you choose, built to the published specifications.
- **Data protection.** The Personal Data Protection Law (Royal Decree 6/2022) has been fully enforceable since 5 February 2026, overseen by the Ministry of Transport, Communications and Information Technology. A data protection officer is mandatory, breaches must be reported within 72 hours, and administrative fines reach OMR 2,000 per violation ([CMS](https://cms.law/en/omn/legal-updates/oman-personal-data-protection-law-entering-the-enforcement-phase)). Health, biometric and genetic data needs a permit, and the penalty for unlawfully transferring data abroad can reach OMR 500,000 ([Clyde & Co](https://www.clydeco.com/fr/insights/2022/2/data-protection-law-issued-in-oman)).
- **Payments.** OmanNet is the Central Bank of Oman's payment gateway, and 95% of cards in Oman are OmanNet debit cards ([Checkout.com](https://www.checkout.com/payment-methods/accept/omannet)).

More in [Oman e-invoicing (Fawtara): what your POS needs](/blog/oman-fawtara-e-invoicing).

## Common questions

### Do you have an office in Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain or Oman?

No. We are based in Cairo and work with companies in all four countries remotely, in Arabic or English. The first step is a free 30-minute WhatsApp call, booked in your own time zone.

### Which of these countries require e-invoicing?

Oman, from 1 April 2027 for companies with annual supplies above OMR 5 million and from 1 October 2027 for the rest. Qatar has a draft law that is not in force, Bahrain expects e-invoicing with no official date, and we found no mandate in Kuwait when we checked on 6 November 2026.

### Which payment methods should our store support?

It depends on the country: KNET in Kuwait, BenefitPay in Bahrain, OmanNet in Oman, and Himyan cards on NAPS through the QPay portal in Qatar. The payment gateway you contract with decides how each method is connected.

### What are your working hours in our time?

Sunday to Thursday, 11:00 to 19:00 in Kuwait, Doha and Manama and 12:00 to 20:00 in Muscat, starting and ending one hour earlier from the last Friday of April to the last Thursday of October.

### Do you build interfaces in Arabic and English?

Yes, and in Kuwait it is required: the CITRA decision says terms must be given in both languages. We write Arabic in formal Modern Standard Arabic and build the right-to-left layout from the start.

### Who owns the code?

Your company does. The code, the database, and the hosting, domain and developer accounts are all in its name, and apps are published under your own Apple and Google accounts.
