Gym management software for a gym in Egypt has to do four things well: keep every membership and its end date accurate, stop expired members at the door, track personal training sessions, and remind members to renew before they drift away. Payments, reports and a WhatsApp connection sit on top of those four. If a system handles memberships, freezes and the gate cleanly, most of the daily arguments at reception disappear.

Here is what to check before you choose, and when a custom system makes more sense than a subscription.

## Memberships, freezes and transfers

Each plan needs a price, a duration and its rules, and each member needs one start and end date that every screen agrees on.

- **Plans of different lengths:** monthly, three months, six months and yearly, plus off-peak or ladies-only plans if you run them.
- **Session-based plans:** for example 12 classes to use within two months, with the count going down at every visit.
- **Freezes:** the end date should move forward automatically by the frozen days, with a limit per plan and the reason recorded.
- **Transfers and upgrades:** moving a member to a longer plan should credit what they already paid, not start a new sale typed by hand.

Ask any vendor to show a freeze in the demo. If the receptionist simply edits the end date, every freeze is a chance for a mistake or a favour.

## Access control at the gate

A membership system that does not control the door depends on the receptionist remembering faces. Access control links the gate or turnstile to the membership, so an expired, frozen or unpaid member is stopped automatically and the reception screen shows why.

Common options are a QR code on the member's phone, an RFID card or wristband, or a fingerprint or face reader. Each check-in is recorded, so you also see who has not come for two weeks and which hours are crowded. Check that the gate keeps working if the internet drops, and that the reader you plan to buy can actually talk to the software.

## Trainer sessions and personal training

The system should sell PT packages (for example 8 or 16 sessions), book each session with a specific trainer, and deduct it when the member checks in or the trainer confirms it.

Trainers should see their own schedule and remaining sessions per client on a phone. The owner should see sessions completed per trainer each month, because that number usually drives commission. If you pay trainers a salary plus commission, the figures should flow into payroll rather than being counted again at the end of the month; our guide to [HR, attendance and payroll software in Egypt](/blog/hr-attendance-payroll-software-egypt) covers that side.

## Renewals and reminders over WhatsApp

Most members do not cancel; they simply forget to renew. A reminder a few days before the end date, and another just after it, brings some of them back. In Egypt, WhatsApp is where members will actually read it.

Use the official WhatsApp Business Platform for this, not tools that automate a personal number. Under WhatsApp's rules, a business needs the member's opt-in before messaging them, and outside the 24-hour window after the member's last message it can only send pre-approved template messages. A renewal reminder is a template; once the member replies, your team or a bot can answer freely. The same connection can send a payment link or a freeze confirmation. ## Payments: cash, cards, wallets and links

Gyms in Egypt take money in several ways at once: cash at reception, cards on a terminal, mobile wallets and instant bank transfers, and sometimes instalments for yearly plans. The software should record every payment against a membership, whatever the method, and close each shift with a cash count, the same way a [POS cashier system for a shop](/blog/pos-cashier-system-for-shops-egypt) should.

If members can renew online or from a WhatsApp link, you need a payment gateway, and each payment should update the membership automatically through a webhook rather than by someone checking a bank app. Our comparison of [payment gateways in Egypt](/blog/payment-gateways-egypt) covers the options. Supplements and drinks sold at the desk belong in the same system.

## Reports the owner should see every day

These are the reports that change decisions:

| Report | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Expiring this week | Who to call or message before they leave |
| Expired and not renewed | Your real churn, by plan and by month |
| Check-ins by hour | When to schedule staff, classes and cleaning |
| Revenue by method and shift | Whether the cash in the drawer matches the system |
| PT sessions by trainer | Commission, and which trainers keep clients |
| Frozen memberships | Freezes that are being used as free time |

With several branches, each report should work per branch and for all branches together.

## Ready-made subscription or a custom system?

Ready-made gym software is the quickest start and fine for many single-branch gyms. A custom system is worth it when your rules do not fit the template or the monthly fees grow with every branch.

| Question | Ready-made subscription | Custom system |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Time to start | Days | Weeks, depending on scope |
| Your freeze, plan and commission rules | Only if the vendor supports them | Built to your rules |
| Arabic interface and receipts | Varies by product | As you specify |
| Gate hardware | Only the devices they support | Chosen with you |
| Data ownership and export | Check the contract | Your database |
| Cost over time | Monthly fee, often per branch or member | Build cost, then maintenance |

Before you subscribe, ask how you would export your members and payments if you leave. Before a custom build, write down your plan, freeze and commission rules: they become the specification.

## Common questions

### Can the system work with the turnstile I already have?

Often, yes, if the device offers an integration method such as a network interface or an SDK. Share the make and model before you decide, because some devices only work with their own software.

### How should freezes be handled?

Set a maximum number of freeze days per plan, record the reason and who approved it, and let the system move the end date automatically. Avoid letting staff edit end dates directly.

### Can members renew without coming to reception?

Yes. A renewal reminder on WhatsApp can include a payment link, and a confirmed payment can extend the membership automatically and send a receipt.

### Do I need a mobile app for members?

Not at the start. A QR code sent on WhatsApp and a simple web page for bookings cover most needs. A members' app makes sense once you run classes that need booking or have several branches.
