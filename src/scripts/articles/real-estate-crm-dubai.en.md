A real estate CRM for a Dubai brokerage or developer has four jobs: pull every lead from ads, portals, the website and WhatsApp into one record; hand it to the right agent within minutes; keep the conversation, unit and payment plan on that record; and show which agents and campaigns close deals. If a system cannot do this with your own lead sources, it is a contact list, not a CRM.

Here is what to check for each job, and when a ready-made CRM is enough.

## Where Dubai brokerages lose leads

Dubai leads arrive from paid ads, property portals, the website, calls, walk-ins and WhatsApp, often from buyers abroad. The usual leaks are:

- **Leads sitting in a portal inbox or email folder** until someone checks it.
- **Leads answered from an agent's personal WhatsApp**, so the history leaves when the agent does.
- **Duplicate records** when one buyer enquires about three listings on two portals.
- **No owner.** A lead posted in a group chat belongs to no one.
- **No source.** The deal closes, but nobody knows which campaign or portal produced it.

## Capture and route every lead

### One record per person

Every enquiry should land in the CRM automatically and be matched to an existing contact by phone number or email, so the agent sees one person with all their enquiries. Ask vendors to show the duplicate check working, not describe it.

### How each source connects

| Source | How it usually reaches the CRM | What to confirm |
|---|---|---|
| Meta lead ads | A webhook from Meta, then the CRM fetches the lead by its ID | Your Page is connected and the form fields map to CRM fields |
| Property portals | The portal's own API where it offers one; otherwise lead emails read into the CRM | Which portals you use and what access each one gives you |
| Website forms | Sent straight to the CRM | The listing, project and campaign travel with the form |
| WhatsApp | The WhatsApp Business Platform connected to the CRM | Every message is tied to the contact record |
| Calls and walk-ins | Entered by the agent or reception | A short form that takes seconds to fill |

Meta's lead ads webhooks send a notice within a few minutes of a lead being created, and the CRM then retrieves its details. Property Finder runs a developer network, PFDN, with APIs for CRMs and developers to manage listings and leads; access is arranged with Property Finder as a partner. For any other portal, ask what integration it offers before assuming one exists.

### Routing rules

Decide who gets which lead: by project or area, by language, by listing agent, or in rotation. Then add a time limit: if the agent does not respond in time, the lead moves to the next agent or a manager. Speed matters most with portal leads, since a portal buyer may have enquired with several agencies.

## WhatsApp follow-up without losing the history

Most buyer conversations happen on WhatsApp, so the CRM should hold them, not the agent's phone. Through the WhatsApp Business Platform, the company number connects to the CRM, every message is saved on the client's record, and a manager can reassign a conversation without losing any of it.

One rule shapes follow-up. When a client messages you, a 24-hour customer service window opens and resets with each new client message. Inside it, agents reply freely; outside it, the business can only send approved templates. A good CRM shows whether the window is open and keeps templates ready: a new listing, a viewing reminder, a price change.

## Units, listings and payment plans

For a developer, or a broker selling off-plan, the CRM should know the inventory as well as the client:

- **Every unit** with its project, type, size, view and price, and a clear status: available, on hold, reserved or sold.
- **Holds that expire**, so a unit returns to available automatically.
- **Payment plans** per project (booking, construction instalments, handover) with each client's payments received and next due date.
- **Documents** such as reservation forms and receipts on the deal record.

For secondary-market listings, the record holds the owner, asking price and the portals showing it. Our [real estate CRM guide for Egypt](/blog/real-estate-crm-egypt) goes deeper into off-plan payment plans.

## Agent performance you can trust

The reports that change decisions are simple:

- **Response time** per agent, from the lead's arrival to the first reply.
- **Pipeline by stage**: new, contacted, viewing, offer, deal and lost, with the reason a lead was lost.
- **Source to deal**: which campaign, portal or listing produced the deals, not just the leads.
- **Activity**: calls, messages and viewings logged per agent per week.

These numbers are only as honest as the data under them. If agents can skip stages or close leads without a reason, the dashboard looks healthy while deals slip away; see [Your dashboard is lying to you](/blog/your-dashboard-is-lying-to-you).

## Client data and invoices

A CRM holds names, phone numbers, ID copies and financial details: personal data from day one. The UAE's Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law 45/2021) has applied on the mainland since January 2022, though its implementing regulations have not been formally issued; DIFC and ADGM have their own data protection laws. In practice, limit who can see and export contacts, log access, and agree in the contract how data is returned or deleted at the end. This is general information, not legal advice.

A brokerage invoicing a developer for commission issues a business-to-business invoice. UAE B2B e-invoicing through an accredited service provider (ASP) goes live on 1 January 2027 for revenue of AED 50 million or more, and on 1 July 2027 for everyone else. If your CRM issues those invoices, check how its data will reach your ASP; see [UAE e-invoicing deadlines for SMEs](/blog/uae-e-invoicing-deadlines-smes).

## Ready-made CRM or custom build

A ready-made real estate CRM is right when your process matches how it works and it already connects to your portals and ad platforms. You pay a monthly fee per user and start quickly. Ask for a trial with your own lead sources, not a demo account.

A custom CRM makes sense when the process is your advantage: a developer with its own payment plan rules, a brokerage with routing no product supports, or a team that wants the CRM, website, WhatsApp and accounting in one system. You own the code and data; the trade-off is build time and the need for a reliable maintenance partner. Either way, make sure you can export all contacts, conversations and deals at any time.

## Common questions

### Can agents keep using WhatsApp?

Yes, through the company number connected to the CRM, not personal phones, so messages stay on the client's record when an agent leaves.

### Why can't my agents message an old lead freely on WhatsApp?

Free-form messages are allowed only within 24 hours of the client's last message. After that, only approved templates can be sent.

### Does the CRM need to support Arabic?

If your clients or staff write in Arabic, yes: the interface, templates and search should handle Arabic names and right-to-left text.

### How long does a custom real estate CRM take to build?

It depends on scope. Lead capture, routing, WhatsApp and the pipeline is a much smaller first version than one adding inventory, payment plans and invoicing. A written quote after a call sets out scope and timeline.
