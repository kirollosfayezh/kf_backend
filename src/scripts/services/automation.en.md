In most companies, hours every week go to work a computer could do: copying orders from one system to another, building the same report every Monday, reminding people about approvals and chasing missing documents.

We find those tasks and automate them, so your team spends its time on the work that needs a person.

## What we automate

- **Data moving between tools**: website forms into your CRM, orders into your accounting system, stock updates between branches.
- **Reports** built and sent automatically every day or week.
- **Reminders and follow-ups**: overdue invoices, expiring contracts, missed tasks.
- **Approvals** that route themselves to the right person and record the decision.
- **Document processing**: turning invoices, forms and emails into structured data, with AI where it helps.
- **Scheduled checks and alerts**, like the random audits and secure store transfers in [Random Audit](/projects/random-audit).

## Reliable, not fragile

An automation nobody trusts gets switched off. Every automation we build includes:

- **Logs** of what ran, when, and what it did.
- **Alerts** when something fails, sent to the right person.
- **A human in the loop** for decisions with real consequences — the system prepares, a person approves.
- **Clear ownership**, documented so your team knows what runs and why.

## How an automation project runs

1. **Find the time sinks.** We list the repetitive tasks and estimate the hours each one costs.
2. **Pick the first win.** High volume, low risk, easy to measure.
3. **Build and run it alongside the manual process** until the results match.
4. **Switch over** and move to the next task.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do we need new software to automate?

Usually not. Most automations connect the tools you already use — your accounting package, CRM, spreadsheets, email and WhatsApp.

### What if an automation makes a mistake?

Each automation logs its actions and alerts someone when a step fails. For important decisions, we keep a person approving the final step.

### How quickly can we see results?

A first automation is often running within two to four weeks, and the time saved is visible immediately.
