Software companies in Egypt come in four kinds: large firms that serve enterprises, small and mid-sized studios that many people call a "software house", marketing agencies that offer development on the side, and freelance developers. Which one you need depends on the size of your project and on how much support you want after delivery, not on a company's place in a "best software company" list.

Last checked: 25 November 2026

## The kinds of software company in Egypt

- **Large firms.** Teams in the hundreds, working mostly with banks, government bodies and organisations that buy through tenders. Formal process, longer timelines, higher cost.
- **Small and mid-sized studios (software houses).** A team of a few engineers up to a few dozen. You talk to the people who write the code, and scope changes more easily. This kind suits most small and mid-sized businesses.
- **Marketing agencies that offer development.** A fit when the website is part of an advertising campaign. Ask who actually does the development: the agency's own team or someone it subcontracts.
- **Freelance developers.** The lowest cost for a small, well-defined job. The risk is continuity: who takes over if they get busy or stop?

## Which kind suits your project

- **A company website or landing page:** a capable freelancer or a small studio.
- **An online store or booking system:** a software studio, because payments, shipping and support after launch need a team, not one person.
- **A mobile app for Android and iPhone:** an app development company with published apps you can download and try.
- **A POS, inventory or ERP system:** a company that knows [how to connect a POS to the ETA e-invoicing system](/blog/eta-e-invoicing-pos-integration), because a mistake here shows up in front of the tax authority, not only in front of a customer.
- **An enterprise project that goes to tender:** a large firm with the registrations and guarantees the buyer asks for.

## Seven questions that show the difference before you sign

- **Who will actually write the code?** The company's own team, or someone else the work is passed to?
- **Can I see similar work running right now?** A link to a live site or an app you can install, not pictures in a presentation.
- **Who owns the source code and the accounts at handover?** The domain, the hosting and the app store accounts should be in your name.
- **Is the price fixed and in writing?** And what counts as out of scope and is billed separately?
- **What is the support plan after launch?** How long it lasts, what it covers and what it costs once the warranty period ends.
- **How do I follow progress?** A test version you see every week beats one delivery at the end.
- **What happens if delivery is late?** The answer should be a clause in the contract, not a spoken promise.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best software company in Egypt?

No single company is the best for every project. The best one for yours has built something similar, shows you that work running and writes the price, the scope and the code ownership into the contract. Lists that rank companies are usually written by one of the companies on them.

### What is the difference between a software company and a software house?

There is no difference in meaning. "Software house" is a common name in Egypt for a small or mid-sized software company that builds software to a client's order.

### Should I hire a software company or a freelance developer?

A freelancer suits a small job with clear limits. If the project will run for years, or touches payments, stock or e-invoicing, a company is safer because the work does not depend on one person.
