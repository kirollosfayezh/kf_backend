Arabic SEO in Saudi Arabia means writing pages in the words Saudi customers actually type, in clear Modern Standard Arabic, on fast pages that Google can read. Google handles 96.1% of searches in Saudi Arabia and 95.42% in the UAE, so Google is the engine to plan for. If your business has no staffed address in the Gulf, you compete in the ordinary (organic) results, not in the map listings.

This guide is for business owners who sell to Saudi and Gulf customers and want their website to show up when those customers search.

## Google is the search engine that matters

Statcounter's figures for October 2026 put Google at 96.1% of search in Saudi Arabia and 95.42% in the UAE. Every other engine shares what is left. In practice that means:

- Follow Google's own guidance (Google Search Central) rather than tricks aimed at other engines.
- Use Google Search Console as your main measuring tool, because it shows what Google actually sees and shows for your site.
- Build clean, crawlable pages first. The same pages are what AI assistants read, as we explain in [how to get found on Google, ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude](/blog/get-found-on-google-chatgpt-gemini).

## Use the words Saudis type, not the words you use

The most common mistake is writing in the vocabulary of the owner or the agency instead of the customer. Google Trends for Saudi Arabia over the 12 months to 6 November 2026 shows how wide the gap can be:

| Term | Saudi interest | Alternative | Saudi interest |
|---|---|---|---|
| «جوال» (mobile phone) | 82 | «موبايل» | 16 |
| «متجر إلكتروني» (online store) | 69 | «متجر أونلاين» | 1 |

Trends scores are relative (0 to 100 within one comparison), not search counts, but the direction is clear. A page about a phone app that only says «موبايل» misses the word most Saudis use. A writer from Egypt or the Levant will often reach for «موبايل» and «أونلاين» first, so this matters most when your copy is written outside the Gulf.

How to apply it:

1. List the 10 to 20 phrases a customer would type to find your service, in Arabic.
2. Compare close variants in Google Trends with the country set to Saudi Arabia, then to the UAE, Kuwait and so on.
3. Use the stronger term in the page title, the main heading and the first paragraph.
4. Use the other variants naturally once or twice further down, never as a keyword list.

Show prices in the local currency by name, such as ريال سعودي or درهم إماراتي, rather than in a currency from another market.

## Write in clear Modern Standard Arabic

Formal Modern Standard Arabic (MSA) reads as professional in every Gulf country, while the dialect of another country reads as foreign. Customers may type in their own dialect, but a clear MSA page that answers their question still serves them. Keep sentences short, put the answer near the top, and write brand and product names the way your customers know them.

Do not publish machine translation from English without a human edit. It produces correct-looking words in an unnatural order, and terms that nobody searches for.

## Give each language its own page

Google's guidance for multilingual sites is to give each language version its own URL, link the versions to each other with hreflang annotations, and avoid redirecting visitors automatically by language or location. For a Gulf business that usually means:

- An Arabic page and an English page for each service, each at its own address.
- hreflang tags that point each page to its version in the other language.
- A real right-to-left layout for Arabic, not an English design with Arabic text dropped in.
- A language switch that the visitor controls.

Google describes country-code domains such as .sa or .ae as a clear signal of the target country. They are not required: many businesses serve several Gulf countries from one .com with Arabic and English pages.

## The map pack needs a real address

The map with a few local businesses that appears for searches like "near me" comes from Google Business Profiles. Google's guidelines only allow a profile for a business that meets customers in person, at its own address or at theirs, and say not to create one for a virtual office unless it is staffed during business hours.

So a company that serves Saudi clients remotely, from Cairo or anywhere else, should not invent a Riyadh address. That breaks Google's rules. Its route is the organic results: clear service pages, useful articles, and an about page that says honestly where you are and whom you serve. If you do have a staffed branch in the Gulf, create a profile for it; the steps are the same as in our guide to [getting found on Google Maps in Egypt](/blog/google-maps-business-profile-egypt).

## Track each country in Search Console

The Performance report in Google Search Console lets you filter clicks, impressions and average position by country, page and query. Check these views every month:

- **Country**: Saudi Arabia, then the UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain and Oman, one at a time.
- **Page**: your Arabic pages against your English pages, to see which language each country uses.
- **Query**: the searches per country, to find questions you have not answered yet.

If a page gets many impressions in Saudi Arabia but few clicks, rewrite its title and description. If a query keeps appearing that no page answers, that is your next article. ## Common questions

### Should a Saudi website be in Arabic or English?

Usually both. Arabic pages reach customers who search in Arabic, and English pages reach those who search in English, including many business buyers. After launch, the country and page filters in Search Console show which language each market actually uses.

### Do I need a .sa or .ae domain?

No. A country-code domain is a clear signal for one country, but a .com with good Arabic and English pages can serve several Gulf countries. Choose based on where you sell, then stay with one structure.

### Can I appear on Google Maps in Riyadh without an office there?

Not within Google's rules. A Business Profile needs a location where you meet customers, or an area you serve in person. A virtual office that is not staffed does not qualify. Remote businesses compete in the organic results instead.

### How long does Arabic SEO take to show results?

Nobody can honestly promise rankings or dates. New pages have to be crawled, indexed and compared with what already ranks, so judge progress over months in Search Console, not over days.

### Does this also help with ChatGPT and Gemini?

Yes, in the same direction. AI assistants draw on clear, crawlable pages that answer specific questions, which is exactly what this guide builds.
